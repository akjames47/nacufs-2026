How Dispatch Goods works

Reusables, finally done right.

A closed-loop system designed from the ground up for high-volume campus environments. No containers lost. No operational headaches. Just results.

01

Reusable Containers Replace Single-Use

Dispatch Goods reusable containers and cups are deployed directly into your dining operation — replacing disposables at the point of service. No student sign-up, no deposits, no penalties. Students simply use them.

02

QR Codes Identify Every Container

Each container and cup has a unique QR code linked to its own geo-fenced landing page. When a student scans it, they're guided back to the nearest return point — making the return process intuitive and frictionless without any app download or account required.

03

Containers Are Returned at Drop Points

Convenient return stations are placed throughout campus — dining halls, residence halls, libraries. Students drop containers off when they're done. No fees, no penalties, no friction.

04

Dispatch Goods Hauls Used, Delivers Clean & Reports

On every service visit, Dispatch Goods picks up used containers and simultaneously delivers a fresh supply of commercially sanitized ones — so your operation never runs dry. Your staff handles none of the logistics. You receive real-time data on waste diverted, containers in circulation, and cost savings — report-ready for STARS submissions and sustainability reporting.
